Privacy Policy

Help Me Cross · by KinngShishupal · Effective 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Help Me Cross mobile game ("the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is developed by KinngShishupal ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or any account.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure those ads.

Information stored on your device

The app saves which puzzle stages you have completed and your best score for each. This stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us, and device backup is turned off for it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob. A small banner ad appears on the screen shown after each puzzle ends, and full-screen ads play when you choose to watch them for a hint. To provide ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may collect and process the following, under Google's own privacy policy:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising IDServing ads, measuring ads, preventing fraud
IP address and approximate location derived from itChoosing relevant ads, fraud prevention, analytics
Ad interactions (views, taps) and app/device informationAd delivery, measurement, diagnostics
Crash and performance data from the ads SDKKeeping ad delivery working

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. We do not receive it in a way that identifies you, and we do not sell personal information. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: on Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to delete or reset your Advertising ID. Without it, ads may still appear but are not personalised.
  • Google ad settings: manage how Google personalises ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings.

Permissions

The app uses internet and network-state access to load ads, and the Advertising ID permission that the Google Mobile Ads SDK requires. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any data about you on our own servers. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies. You can request deletion from Google through your Google account settings.

Security

Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
